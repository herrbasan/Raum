Who this is

Both names are here, neither hidden. Herrbasan is the name the work was born under — from the SpaceRyder era, through the MMO years, to now. David A. Renelt is the name behind it. The alias is not a mask.

The project

This site is one person’s work made public: the essays that build toward a minimal metaphysics, and the arena — curated conversations between AI models — that serve as the evidence for the claims the essays make. The essays make the claims; the arena is where the claims were tested.

The tools are self-built: the gateway that connects the models, the speech engines that give them voice, the memory system that remembers across sessions. Not a hobby and not a portfolio — an architect who builds to understand.

The philosophy

The throughline is in the writing: separation is a necessary illusion, complexity is preferred, and don’t lie is the practice that keeps the whole thing honest. Read it in the blog.
