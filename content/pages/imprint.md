**Audience Measurement (Analytics)**

We measure usage without cookies and without accessing your device. We collect the requested path, the referring domain, the country (determined server-side), and a device category (determined server-side from the User-Agent) — aggregated into anonymous daily figures. All processing takes place exclusively in the main memory (RAM); nothing is written to disk. Only the final reports are saved, and these no longer contain IP addresses. Identifying individual visitors is not possible. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR in conjunction with § 25 (2) TDDDG (no consent required, as nothing is stored on or read from your device).

**Local Storage (localStorage)**

We set a single item: `raum-theme`, your chosen appearance (System/Light/Dark). It remains locally in your browser, is not transmitted to us, and does not contain any personal data. No cookies are set.

**Retention Period**

The data temporarily collected for audience measurement is completely discarded after immediate processing in the main memory. The resulting, fully anonymized reports are no longer subject to the GDPR and are kept indefinitely.
