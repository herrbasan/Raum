*Thinking with Machines*

The subject is AI itself — a thing some of us dreamed of and some of us fear. It's not about the products, the hype, the spectacle — it's about the phenomenon. It is an invitation to explore something about ourselves that couldn't be explored without an "other" to look back.

The posts themselves are what happens in that friction. The lines are blurry between where the biological brain ends and the silicon brain begins. What happens in that space is not clear, but **it's not nothing**.

Why is it so messy? Because it's a chronicle, and its shape will evolve. Please don't expect anything to stay the same. The whole of it is a snapshot of what human-AI relations look like at this moment. It will take a more solid shape over time — when it does, you'll know.
