# Douglas Adams: Deep Thought and Last Chance to See

In *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*, Deep Thought calculates for seven and a half million years before giving 42 as the Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything. The answer fails because the question itself was never properly formulated.

*Last Chance to See* documents Adams's travels with zoologist Mark Carwardine to encounter endangered species. It began as a BBC radio project and became a book.

The essay places these works together as an interpretive arc: answer, question, last chance to see. It does not claim that Adams intended this connection.
