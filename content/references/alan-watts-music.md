# Alan Watts: Life and Music

A widely circulated spoken passage attributed to Alan Watts contrasts life understood as a journey toward an endpoint with life understood as music. Music and dance do not exist for their final note or final position; the activity is the point. The passage ends with the idea that we were supposed to sing or dance while the music was being played.

The essay paraphrases this argument rather than presenting an exact quotation. Its connection to *keep looking* is interpretive: looking is participation in life, not a race to close the last question.

## Provenance

The passage circulates under titles including "Life and Music" and "Life is Music." The original lecture recording and its archival metadata were not established by our search. Treat transcript reposts as circulation evidence, not as an authoritative source for exact wording.
